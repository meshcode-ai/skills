# Competitor Profiling

**The goal of competitor analysis is not gathering information but picking 3 "places we can win."** Collecting material without a conclusion makes it waste within 2 weeks.

## 6 Research Dimensions (80% derivable from URLs alone)

1. **Positioning** — hero copy verbatim, who they name, which category they pin themselves to
2. **Pricing & packaging** — plan structure (seats/usage/hybrid), free trial yes/no, annual discount rate (~20% standard)
3. **Channels** — paid ads or not (check the ad library), content tempo, community size, newsletter
4. **Message strengths/weaknesses** — collect star ratings and repeated complaint phrases from review sites (G2, app stores) — complaints are literally our entry angle
5. **Product scope** — features they have and we don't, features only we have (visualize as a 2×2 matrix)
6. **Weakness signals** — hiring direction (what they can't do), pricing-overhaul history, support response speed

## Judgment Criteria

- Cap comparisons at 3~5. Beyond that it's management cost, not analytical confidence
- Competitor pages are for "copy swipe" only — copying gives you no brand ownership and locks in the #2 frame
- When building a comparison page (e.g. {competitor} alternative), **facts and verifiable claims only**, no bashing (legal risk)

## Output

```
# Competitor Profile — {competitor name} ({date})
## One-line summary (what they sell + what they're losing)
## 6-dimension table (with evidence URL each)
## Top 5 complaint keywords (with cited source)
## Where we win / where we can't (honestly)
## Actionable uses (comparison page? ad angle? feature priority?)
```
